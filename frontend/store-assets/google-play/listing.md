### Full description

Anovo brings practical AI writing tools into one focused app. Rewrite sentences, compare contextual alternatives for individual words, and apply the phrasing that best matches your meaning and voice.

Use Anovo to paraphrase and humanize drafts, check grammar, summarize long text, translate content, analyse tone, and continue ideas with Co-Writer. Select text to compare multiple sentence rewrites or choose a single word to explore contextual replacements.

Anovo includes light and dark themes, native sharing, deep links, and clear connectivity feedback. Accounts are optional for core writing tools, and account deletion is available in the app and on the web.
